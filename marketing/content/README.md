# Yard launch content drafts

`launch-drafts.json` contains prelaunch Instagram and Facebook copy for Yard, an app being built to make discovering Charlotte events and seeing friends’ plans feel more connected.

## How to use these drafts

- Every post and reel is marked `draft`. The 30-day calendar uses relative day numbers only; it is a content outline, not a schedule.
- Three feed posts are marked `pinnedCandidate`. Review the final profile layout before choosing pins.
- The copy describes supported product behavior in build-stage language. It does not announce a launch date, public availability, event inventory, or a waitlist.
- No customer stories, testimonials, named real events, or traction claims are included. Treat any app screens or sample content used in creative as illustrative; label demo data clearly and never present it as live activity.
- The repository has no verified public website, waitlist, or social-account link. The profile draft intentionally has no external URL. Add one only after ownership and destination are confirmed.
- Confirm event details, permissions for any people or footage, current product behavior, accessibility of graphic text, and platform-specific formatting before publication.

## Brand direction

Use the lowercase `yard.` wordmark and “Find your people.” Warm cream, forest green, concise friendly copy, and Charlotte as the first city are the established direction. Keep graphic text short and legible; use the brand mockup as a visual reference, not as a promise that every pictured screen or event is live.
